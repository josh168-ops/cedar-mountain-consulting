Larry has 30 years of experience as an investment management attorney advising private fund managers and SEC-registered investment advisers on regulatory and compliance issues. He most recently served as General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer for Northgate Capital, a San Francisco venture capital manager, where he oversaw the firm's compliance program. He was previously Vice President & Managing Director of Ascendant Compliance Management's San Francisco office, leading compliance consulting for venture capital, private equity, and hedge fund managers. He has also served as General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer at Bellatore Financial, as Of Counsel at Shartsis Friese LLP, and as a senior associate at K&L Gates LLP. Larry earned his J.D. cum laude from New York Law School and his B.A. from Binghamton University (SUNY).
