Meher has over 15 years of experience in the financial services industry. She was in-house counsel and director of compliance at Western Technology Investment (WTI), a registered investment adviser and private business development company. She has held roles as director of compliance at Duff & Phelps (now Kroll) and as an associate in the private investment funds group at Ropes & Gray LLP in New York. Meher earned her HBSc and J.D. from the University of Toronto.
