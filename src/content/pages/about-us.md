## Our Story

Our experience in law firms and in-house compliance showed us that firms need more than regulatory advice. We founded Cedar Mountain Consulting to pair legal and regulatory depth with practical, hands-on implementation.

**Accountability:** We treat your business as if it were our own, with urgency, ownership, and care.

**Clarity Over Complexity:** We translate complex regulations into clear, actionable insights.

**High-Touch Service:** We prioritize trust, responsiveness, and long-term relationships.

**Strategic Alignment:** We think beyond checklists to align compliance with your business goals.

**Hands-On Execution:** From drafting policies to preparing for an SEC exam, we help implement what we recommend.

**Regulatory Depth:** Our team brings decades of in-house and regulatory experience. We understand how regulations are applied in practice.

**Senior-Led Support:** Every client works directly with experienced senior consultants.

**Tailored and Embedded:** We go beyond templates to build compliance programs that fit your operations.
