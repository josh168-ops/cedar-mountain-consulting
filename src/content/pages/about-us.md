## Our Story

Cedar Mountain Consulting was founded to fill a gap in the compliance consulting industry. Too often, consultants lacked practical experience, regulatory depth, or a real understanding of fund structures and business strategy. We built the consulting firm we wished had existed.

## Our Values

**Accountability:** We treat your business like it is our own - with urgency, ownership, and care.

**Clarity Over Complexity:** We translate complex regulations into clear, actionable insights.

**High-Touch Service:** We prioritize trust, responsiveness, and long-term relationships.

**Strategic Alignment:** We think beyond checklists to align compliance with your business goals.

## Why We Are Different

**Hands-On Execution:** From drafting policies to prepping for an SEC exam, we help implement what we recommend.

**Regulatory Depth:** Our team brings decades of in-house and regulatory experience. We understand how regulations are applied in practice.

**Senior-Led Support:** Every client works directly with experienced senior consultants.

**Tailored and Embedded:** We do not just deliver templates - we build compliance programs that make sense for your operations.
