This Privacy Policy describes how Cedar Mountain Consulting, LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our website (the "Website").

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you submit a contact form or inquiry. We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our Website, including:

We use the information we collect to:

## How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with service providers, business partners, and vendors who assist us in operating our Website and conducting our business. We may also share information in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition.
