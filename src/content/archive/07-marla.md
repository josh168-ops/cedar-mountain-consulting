Marla Wooton, CRCP®, is a senior compliance executive and consultant with more than 30 years of experience across SEC-registered investment advisers, broker-dealers, and wealth management firms. She helps organizations build practical compliance, supervision, governance, and operational programs that protect clients and support responsible growth.

As former Chief Compliance Officer of Composition Wealth, Marla led regulatory strategy, SEC and DOL oversight, compliance testing, examinations, and acquired-firm integration during significant growth. Her experience also spans operations, client service, brokerage and advisory operations, trading, portfolio administration, broker-dealer supervision, and high-net-worth client service. Earlier in her career, she held leadership roles at Bank of America Securities, Deutsche Bank Alex. Brown, Themis Asset Strategies, and Bel Air Investment Advisors.

Marla is a Certified Regulatory Compliance Professional and maintains eligible FINRA qualifications through the Maintaining Qualifications Program. She was nominated for the 2023 Los Angeles Times Inspirational Women Award and spoke on SEC examination readiness at the 2024 NSCP National Conference.
